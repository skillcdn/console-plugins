## 0.3.0

### Minor Changes

- [`5234d53`](https://github.com/skillcdn/console-plugins/commit/5234d533676f481ba89dcb54fd1267d9af2bbc5b) Thanks [@samo-lucid](https://github.com/samo-lucid)! - Breaking: how approved leave is cancelled is each kind's to say, and the configuration's one rule goes, with `configure --cancellation`. A kind has a deadline of the notice's shape: none, some days before, a time the day before, or a time on the first day, in the workspace's zone. By it, the person cancels at once, at once up to some days and by a decision beyond them, by a decision, or not at all, HR alone; after it, through the first day, by a decision or by HR; once the first day has gone by, HR alone cancels, under every rule, since days were used. A decision goes to those the leave's first step asked, or to the person's manager where it was approved as written, and the person's own answer does not allow it. HR sets the rule on the Setup page, under Leave types, in a part of its own after the requests, or with `type <key> --cancel-notice --cancel --cancel-late`, which reads it back with the rest; a new kind, and each kind `setup` writes, is cancelled at once by the deadline its requests keep, or until 23:59 the day before where they keep none, so that no day begun is given back unseen, and by a decision after it. A request still waiting is cancelled at once, and HR cancels anything at once, as before.
  
  The upgrade, to data version 3, writes on every kind what the configuration said, so that nothing changes by it but one thing. `free` is cancelled at once until 23:59 the day before and by HR from the first day, as it was; `hr` by HR alone; `approval` by a decision, except leave whose first day has gone by, which is now HR's to cancel rather than a manager's decision. Read each kind's rule on the Setup page after upgrading, and change what the organization does otherwise: "at once until 09:00 on the day, the manager after that, HR from the next day" is `--cancel-notice sameDay --cancel-notice-time 09:00 --cancel now --cancel-late decision`.
  
  A request as the queries and the api answer it says what cancelling it would come to for its reader, `cancellation`: `now`, `decision` with `cancelDeciders`, whom the decision would ask, or `null` where they may not; `cancellable` stays. My leave says it before the cancellation is confirmed.

- [`a342bfd`](https://github.com/skillcdn/console-plugins/commit/a342bfd6b638adad99573db3b226b9953421c776) Thanks [@samo-lucid](https://github.com/samo-lucid)! - Breaking: a manager's reach is held to what the design says. Under `--late refuse`, a request past the notice is refused to a manager writing for their people too, and HR alone writes it; `manager` leaves it to a manager or HR, as before. A plugin calling for a person stands for them as a member, since the console's `manager` follows the reporting line and is never a plugin's: through a plugin nobody reads or records the leave of the people below them, and `requests --scope team` answers HR the people below them too, `all` being everyone's. The records of the collection `people` are HR's alone through the console's routes, since they hold HR's note; a person reads what is theirs through the queries, as the pages always did. Someone named in an exception stands in the line of the people of its teams alone.
  
  Fixed: a person's own answer allowing the cancellation of their leave keeps the leave, as their own answer to its request sends it back. A configuration brought from 0.1.0 straight to this version keeps its rule of cancelling, which the upgrade lost where an exception had no teams. What a person wrote, a reason or a name, reads in a decision as words on one line, never as a line of the plugin's own, a link or emphasis. A text a spreadsheet would take for a formula is written as text in the export. A manager writing for someone sees their balance in `preview`, and in the refusal of too few days, only where the configuration shows it to them. Leave whose cancellation waits stays on the calendar. A cancellation is written before its days come back, so that two at once never give days back for leave that stands. Cancelling at once up to no days is refused, as carrying over up to none is. The ledger reads a reversal after the line it undoes, each with the balance it left. A refusal past a kind's deadline no longer says that the person could have cancelled leave HR alone cancels. The Korean of the ledger names an accrual without calling it monthly and a payout as owed. `type` reads a kind back with what a request for days gone by meets where it has no notice, and `adjust` answers with the line's id, which `reverse` takes. The descriptions of the commands, the queries and the api say their defaults and what they answer more exactly.

## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- [`83f314f`](https://github.com/skillcdn/console-plugins/commit/83f314f329db613ddb08a26cd567df2daf37efda) Thanks [@samo-lucid](https://github.com/samo-lucid)! - Breaking: built for console 0.4.6 or later. The pages Approvals and People and the widget Approvals are a manager's and HR's: the role `manager` follows who reports to whom in the console and is granted by nobody, so that someone who manages nobody no longer sees them. A decision reaches whoever it is for and the managers above them wherever they are, and the pages open a decision at its own address. Nobody is added to the board project any more, to request leave or to approve it: a request raises its decision there whether the requester may see the project or not, and they read that decision as the one who raised it. The queries `requests` and `request-view`, and the call `request-view`, answer a decision's address as `null` where the console does not show the caller the decision, so that a page offers no address that answers not found; the ids stay. The task to mend a line that a request found broken waits in the new collection `chores` until the daily job writes it, since a call for a person writes no task on the board. `setup` says that the workspace keeps UTC only where nobody set its zone. A day the calendar does not have is the console's to refuse. A link in an announcement to the web opens in a new tab, and one to the console goes there, by the console's whole address too.

- [`83f314f`](https://github.com/skillcdn/console-plugins/commit/83f314f329db613ddb08a26cd567df2daf37efda) Thanks [@samo-lucid](https://github.com/samo-lucid)! - Another plugin may call `hr` for a person who acted elsewhere, a messenger say: the call is that person's, in their language and within what both may do, and a request made so names the plugin it came through as `requestedVia`, on the pages, in `requests` and in `request-view`. The skill `hr-administration` asks for the organization's rule first, sets it up in order and compares each answer with it: a rule of the whole organization is the policy's, and an adjustment is one person's.
