# @skillcdn/console-plugin-hr

## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- [`83f314f`](https://github.com/skillcdn/console-plugins/commit/83f314f329db613ddb08a26cd567df2daf37efda) Thanks [@samo-lucid](https://github.com/samo-lucid)! - Breaking: built for console 0.4.6 or later. The pages Approvals and People and the widget Approvals are a manager's and HR's: the role `manager` follows who reports to whom in the console and is granted by nobody, so that someone who manages nobody no longer sees them. A decision reaches whoever it is for and the managers above them wherever they are, and the pages open a decision at its own address. Nobody is added to the board project any more, to request leave or to approve it: a request raises its decision there whether the requester may see the project or not, and they read that decision as the one who raised it. The queries `requests` and `request-view`, and the call `request-view`, answer a decision's address as `null` where the console does not show the caller the decision, so that a page offers no address that answers not found; the ids stay. The task to mend a line that a request found broken waits in the new collection `chores` until the daily job writes it, since a call for a person writes no task on the board. `setup` says that the workspace keeps UTC only where nobody set its zone. A day the calendar does not have is the console's to refuse. A link in an announcement to the web opens in a new tab, and one to the console goes there, by the console's whole address too.

- [`83f314f`](https://github.com/skillcdn/console-plugins/commit/83f314f329db613ddb08a26cd567df2daf37efda) Thanks [@samo-lucid](https://github.com/samo-lucid)! - Another plugin may call `hr` for a person who acted elsewhere, a messenger say: the call is that person's, in their language and within what both may do, and a request made so names the plugin it came through as `requestedVia`, on the pages, in `requests` and in `request-view`. Leave is never recorded as approved outright through another plugin. A plugin calling as itself holds the roles granted to it and is no person: it reads what those roles read, and requests, cancels and previews nothing.

- [`29a1db6`](https://github.com/skillcdn/console-plugins/commit/29a1db6838ae98bddd55a0ed186e99f5a63afa93) Thanks [@samo-lucid](https://github.com/samo-lucid)! - The setting commands say what they saved. `setup` lists what the preset chose that organizations set differently, the year and its first period, the first year's monthly days, what is left at a period's end, how annual leave is requested and approved, the line and the time zone, for HR to compare with their rule before enrolling anyone; `policy`, `type` and `configure` read the policy, the kind and the line back in sentences, what a late request meets under each way of approving included; `enroll --leave on` says what it wrote, what is available, and where first-year monthly days came from. The skill `hr-administration` asks for the organization's rule first, sets it up in order and compares each answer with it: a rule of the whole organization is the policy's, and an adjustment is one person's.

- [`29a1db6`](https://github.com/skillcdn/console-plugins/commit/29a1db6838ae98bddd55a0ed186e99f5a63afa93) Thanks [@samo-lucid](https://github.com/samo-lucid)! - A person's lines follow a policy that changed: `recount <login>` reverses the lines the policy wrote and no longer owes, or owes otherwise, with the expiries and payouts that closed them, each by a line of its own, and writes what the policy now owes. A line written with `adjust` is undone with `reverse <line> --note <why>`, and the ledger says which lines were reversed. Both are HR's, on a person's page too. Someone enrolled with first-year monthly days the organization does not give is set right with `policy <key> --monthly off`, then `recount <login>`, and `reverse` for each adjustment that offset those days.

- [`83f314f`](https://github.com/skillcdn/console-plugins/commit/83f314f329db613ddb08a26cd567df2daf37efda) Thanks [@samo-lucid](https://github.com/samo-lucid)! - The console's teams. The calendar's `team` is the people of a person's teams, or those who share their manager for someone in no team. An exception of the line may hold for teams, `"teams"` in `configure --rules` and a choice on the Setup page. An announcement may be for teams, `announcement --audience <team key>,...` and a choice in the editor, and only those teams and HR read it, its line in the lists naming them; `--audience everyone` makes it everyone's again. A team is named by its key, as `console teams` lists it, and a team the console removes stays named where it was, holding for nobody, until HR takes it off. The upgrade to `dataVersion` 2 gives what exists no team.

- [`29a1db6`](https://github.com/skillcdn/console-plugins/commit/29a1db6838ae98bddd55a0ed186e99f5a63afa93) Thanks [@samo-lucid](https://github.com/samo-lucid)! - What other plugins hear of `hr` and may call is its contract, written in the README's "The contract" and held to the code by a test: the events, each with its data and the order they come in; an `api` for a plugin with `plugins:hr` (`request-view`, `types`, `balance`, `preview`, `request`, `cancel`), each the command or the query of that name under the caller's roles; and the option keys `approve` and `reject` of every decision. The query `request-view <id>` reads one request in full, for the person, those above them, its line and HR. A cancellation that waits for a decision is the event `leave-cancelling`. A request keeps whom its decision was addressed to where the console sent it on up the line, so that `requests --scope waiting`, Approvals and the overview show it to whoever it waits for: the records move to `dataVersion` 2, and the upgrade writes the field for every request.

### Patch Changes

- [`80de388`](https://github.com/skillcdn/console-plugins/commit/80de388a288ed2b5422c208875c5b3f126b20b64) Thanks [@samo-lucid](https://github.com/samo-lucid)! - A balance adds up when a person's past leave is entered after they were enrolled. An expiry or a payout written before the use counted for the days it said, though those days had already gone: the balance showed them as expired, the ledger listed them, and the year's export gave payroll more paid days than were closed. They now count for what they took, in all three, and the ledger leaves out one that took nothing. `carriedIn` includes what the period before left and still holds, the card on the leave page shows an adjustment, and what a person may take was right before and has not changed.

- [`80de388`](https://github.com/skillcdn/console-plugins/commit/80de388a288ed2b5422c208875c5b3f126b20b64) Thanks [@samo-lucid](https://github.com/samo-lucid)! - The words now say what the rules do. Automatic approval names that `always` takes a late request too, so that a kind whose late requests must reach a person is set to `upTo`; a policy that pays unused days says so in its summary rather than that they lapse. The days and notice times name the workspace's time zone: `config` answers it, Setup shows it, and `setup` says when the workspace keeps UTC. A day the calendar does not have, February 30, is refused by `request` and `preview`.

### Minor Changes

- [`8d4fd89`](https://github.com/skillcdn/console-plugins/commit/8d4fd89eb8706cd759213d548d95aa4f9b044d7d) Thanks [@samo-lucid](https://github.com/samo-lucid)! - Announcements, in the first version of HR: what HR tells everyone, written with a toolbar (headings, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, highlight, lists, quotes, dividers, links) and with pictures, as a draft of the writer's own until it is published, by category, pinned for as long as HR says, on a page of their own, a widget in four sizes and the overview.
