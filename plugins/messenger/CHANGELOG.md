The role `messenger` could read the plugin's records through the console (`console plugin messenger records`), and there whom a message was sent on to and which task or decision it was of, those of projects they may not see among them, and who asked for leave, who decides it and how it ended, which HR shows to them and to HR alone.
  
  - **The records are an administrator's**, all but a member's own choice and what waits for their day's message, which stay theirs. The pages and the commands read none of them and show what they did: `status`, `people`, `routes`, `channels` and `activity` answer whoever keeps the messenger.

  - **The Activity page says what it left unsaid:** how many a notification was sent on to, that nobody the console told takes it in Slack, and a decision answered by its name. A leave event nobody of was in Slack to be told reads as passed over, and no longer as sent.
  
  A line kept by an earlier version is forgotten 30 days after it was written.

- [#22](https://github.com/skillcdn/console-plugins/pull/22) [`86bbb3f`](https://github.com/skillcdn/console-plugins/commit/86bbb3fe96bdf1f7bdc9bc96bac3a3124034c2ff) Thanks [@kdxlabs-erato](https://github.com/kdxlabs-erato)! - Breaking: built for console 0.6.2 or later. The messenger can tell the console which Slack account is which member's, with a new optional capability, `people:accounts`, and an agent account's wake from Slack then says which member wrote and whether one of its supervisors did.
  
  - **Off unless an administrator turns it on**, on the plugin's settings page. An agent account takes a supervisor's own word in Slack as an instruction, and with this capability the console takes a supervisor's Slack account, as the messenger matched it by work email, for that supervisor's: turn it on where the workspace's addresses are held as surely as a sign-in to the console. Without it the messenger says nothing and works as before.
  - **Only a link the matching wrote is said**, by Slack's ids for the workspace and the account. No name, message or command reaches it.
  - **Only of a workspace an administrator of the console accepted.** Of one connected before this version, whoever gave out the link it was installed through is asked after. Where that is no administrator, the Connection page and `status` say so, and an administrator accepts the workspace there or with `workspace <id> --accept`, which tells its accounts at once. Once whoever accepted it administers the console no more, what was said is taken back within the hour.
  - **Kept current:** with every matching, and every hour, what the console keeps is brought to the live links, so that turning the capability on is acted on within the hour, and an account whose link is gone is taken back. A workspace taken away takes with it what was said of it.
  
  The manifest names a capability a console before 0.6.2 does not know, so this version runs on console 0.6.2 or later, before 0.7.0.

- [`31ae3f3`](https://github.com/skillcdn/console-plugins/commit/31ae3f3aa22ec9aed19eab1bf840f10cd80484c7) Thanks [@samo-lucid](https://github.com/samo-lucid)! - Breaking: the messenger sends on to Slack what the console told each member of, and no longer decides for itself who is told. It asks one capability more, `notifications:read`: once this version is installed the plugin waits, disabled, until an administrator consents to it, on the plugin's settings page or with `console plugin messenger capabilities`.
  
  - **Who is told is the console's to say.** From 0.6.1 the console decides who is told of a task, a decision or an agent account, and each member chooses it in the console's own settings (Settings, Notifications; `console notify`; a task's bell). The messenger sends on what the console told, to whom it told it. A person who chose nothing in the console is told of their agent accounts alone, so nobody is sent a task given to them, a move, a comment or a decision in Slack until they turn that kind on in the console. Tell your people so.
  - **An agent account is sent nothing of the board in Slack.** The console wakes it itself for what it is told, and the messenger sends that to nobody, so that an account is no longer told twice.
  - **What a member chooses in the messenger is what reaches Slack.** The page My notifications is My Slack notifications: each kind the console tells them of, in the groups of the console's own settings, is sent on to Slack or kept out of it, as are the tasks whose bell they turned on themselves, at once or in one message a day. A kind that is off in the console reaches Slack by no choice made there.
  - **`notify` takes the console's kinds, by its names:** `assigned`, `ready`, `moved`, `changed`, `commented`, `archived`, `decision`, `answered`, `agent`, and `followed` for the tasks a member turned on. `decisions` and `agents` are `decision` and `agent` now. `--mute`, `--unmute` and `--own-agents` are gone: a project turned off and whether one's own agents count are chosen in the console (`console notify`). `notices` no longer answers `mutedProjects` or `ownAgents`, and `project-notices` answers the workspace the project is told in alone, without `muted`, `workspaces` and `keeper`.
  - **The messenger has no tab on a project.** Turning a project off is the console's, and which of several workspaces a project is told in is said on the Connection page, beside the workspaces, by the messenger role, where there is more than one.
  - **Two kinds are new to Slack:** a task one is at work on is ready to begin, and a decision one raised was answered, with the option chosen. A decision an agent account raised for whom it reports to reads as a decision, also where the console tells it under their agent accounts.
  - **The records move to data version 4**, by the upgrade. A kind a member turned off themselves stays off; a kind that was merely off for whoever never chose follows the console now. The projects a member turned off and whether their own agents count are not carried into the console, which a plugin cannot write: each member chooses them there.
