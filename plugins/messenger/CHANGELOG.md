## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- [`b3f7dad`](https://github.com/skillcdn/console-plugins/commit/b3f7dad9c2ee1c16d388310d4b207c4ab4e0af7b) Thanks [@samo-lucid](https://github.com/samo-lucid)! - The leave shortcut in Slack is named 휴가 in Korean and Time off in English, where it was 휴가 신청 and Request leave, and its form cancels leave as well as requesting it: above the fields it lists the person's leave from today on that HR says they may cancel themselves, the nearest five with a link to the rest on the console, each with a Cancel button. The button opens a second form over the first, which says what cancelling comes to by HR's rules (at once, or a decision and whose) and takes a reason; submitted, it cancels the leave for the person as HR's `cancel` does and says at once what became of it, and the list below is drawn again as the person goes back to it. The English name has no "leave" in it, since Slack's own `/leave` leaves the channel and Slack lists the two together. The cancellation of leave approved as it was written, which had no conversation, now opens one of the person and whoever decides it, so that the person reads what becomes of it there. The list and the rules need HR 0.3.0; with HR 0.2 the form requests leave as before. The app's manifest is written again within the hour of upgrading, with the new names; the shortcut keeps working meanwhile.

- [`d559f09`](https://github.com/skillcdn/console-plugins/commit/d559f09787c9912b0aff58c5ce5c19f1b5acfd2f) Thanks [@samo-lucid](https://github.com/samo-lucid)! - Breaking: built for console 0.4.7 or later, which says the workspace's language to a plugin. The app's names in Slack, its shortcut's among them, and a route's channel read the workspace's language where an administrator set one (`console workspace edit --language ko`), unless whoever connects the app or adds the route chooses another; where nobody set one, the language of whoever connects or adds it, as before. Someone the console's pages have not seen yet, with no language of their own, reads their thread and the leave form in the workspace's language, else the app's. The Connection and Routes pages offer the workspace's language first. An app made before keeps its language: choose another on the Connection page and update the app.

### Patch Changes

- [`687a549`](https://github.com/skillcdn/console-plugins/commit/687a549e4b09de24acdea3a1c109c63788184c7f) Thanks [@samo-lucid](https://github.com/samo-lucid)! - The app's names and icon in Slack are chosen when it is connected: `connect --app-name` names the app, up to 35 characters in any script, and `--bot-name` its bot, in Latin letters, digits, spaces, `.`, `_` and `-` alone, since Slack makes the bot's handle (@) from it and refuses a name it cannot; both are checked before Slack is asked, kept for the next app, and an empty value brings back the language's own ("콘솔" and "console" in Korean). `--icon` gives Slack the public https address of a square image of 512 to 2000 pixels, which Slack fetches; where the configuration token may not set icons, `connect` and the Connection page say where to upload it by hand. The Connection page has the three fields beside the language, and says the bot's handle as Slack made it, read once the app is installed and again when the bot is renamed. A bot renamed on an installed app may keep its handle: disconnect and connect again while nobody uses the app. The records move to data version 2, which the upgrade writes: the connection keeps the names and the icon chosen, none at first.

- [`21fdb87`](https://github.com/skillcdn/console-plugins/commit/21fdb8755dfdd7622c7ac4f7882718fbd5e0a495) Thanks [@samo-lucid](https://github.com/samo-lucid)! - The leave form in Slack follows the kind chosen: annual leave is chosen first where it is offered, as on HR's own form, and choosing another kind draws the form again for it, the days picked kept. A kind taken in whole days offers no part of a day, one taken in halves the two halves, one taken in quarters the halves and the four quarters; its reason is asked, optional or left out as HR says; and with HR 0.3.0 its rules are said under it in HR's own sentence. What the person has left stands at the top of the form, and someone HR keeps no leave for is told so in place of the form, since HR would refuse their request whatever the kind. A refusal is said beside the field it is about, by HR's code. The quarters of a day are named as HR names them: in Korean "오전 전반 반반차" to "오후 후반 반반차", in English "Early-morning quarter" to "Late-afternoon quarter", in the form and in the threads and the channels alike.

- [`16f22aa`](https://github.com/skillcdn/console-plugins/commit/16f22aa4ad86c51db9d2db7abcfcf06e8d9d8f03) Thanks [@samo-lucid](https://github.com/samo-lucid)! - `status` says the next thing to do to set the messenger up, a step at a time: `next` is the first not done of a public address, the configuration token, the app, its installation, HR's role `hr` for the messenger (without which the shortcut works and no thread is begun), people with no email in the console (`noEmail` counts them) and a route, else `ready`; `text` says it in the caller's language, and the Connection page says it at its top. The README's "Enabling it" says beforehand what an installer has to decide or prepare: the app's names and icon, that installing the app is a person's step within fifteen minutes in the browser signed in to the console, how people are matched by their work email and what to do for those with none, which channels take a route, that the skill can be read before enabling, and that a secret is typed unseen at the terminal, never through an agent.

## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- [`8783476`](https://github.com/skillcdn/console-plugins/commit/87834761131f76f95b641ba7c5f02fa6b5a3541b) Thanks [@samo-lucid](https://github.com/samo-lucid)! - The first version of Messenger, built for console 0.4.6 or later: what the console's plugins have for people, told in Slack, and what people do there brought back. Whoever holds the role `messenger` connects the workspace's Slack from the Connection page, with the app configuration token and its refresh token, which an administrator pastes once: the plugin makes the app, the console seals its secrets, and someone who may install apps installs it from Slack's own page. People are matched to their accounts by email, within the hour and every day. Disconnecting uninstalls the app and deletes it, so that the next connection may be another workspace, as from one kept for trying the plugin to the organization's own. For HR 0.2: a shortcut that opens the leave form in Slack, for the presser's own leave; each request threaded with the person and whoever decides, approved or rejected with a button that answers the console's decision for whoever pressed it, on their plate alone; approved leave told to the channels the Routes page names, in the person's name, and struck through when cancelled. The deployment needs a public https address Slack can reach, and outbound https to slack.com and hooks.slack.com; an administrator turns on `plugins:hr` and grants the messenger HR's role `hr` for the threads.
