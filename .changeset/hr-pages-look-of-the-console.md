The pages look like the console's own, and their panes line up. Every pane is titled as the console titles its panes, the annual balance on the overview and each kind on My leave included. The overview sets its panes in rows, each pane beside the one it sits best with and as tall as it, so that the two columns share their edges; HR's counts are said beside the day in the head. Nothing runs across a wide page in one long row any more: requests, on Approvals, My leave and a person's page, are cards side by side, each with where it stands at its top and the way to its decision at its foot; every announcement is a card, the pinned first, and HR's drafts follow in a pane of their own, open; the overview's lists flow into columns, the exceptions of the approval line are cards, a setting's words stand next to its control, and the year's holidays read down as many columns as fit. The form that requests leave keeps a comfortable width, with what the request would come to in a column beside it, parted by a line rather than boxed, and My leave sets the person's requests beside it, exactly as tall as it and scrolling within it when they are many, and their terms in the row of their balances, a card as tall as those. A kind's card carries the kind's colour, a wash over its head, with the figures under its bar as small stats, a dot in the bar's colour before the ones it draws, and the days about to go in a pill; and the ledger reads as a bank statement does: the kind of line is chosen with chips, each day is a heading with its weekday, and under it a row per line, what it was as its title ("Annual leave granted", "Unused annual leave paid out", "Annual leave used") with the detail under it, and at the right the days it moved, signed, with the balance it left beneath, a line given back struck through; a day that hands out a period's days says a new period begins. The query `ledger` answers each line's `after`, the days left once it was written, for that. The Korean words of the ledger and the balances say what happened in everyday terms: a kind's period is written with its years, the figures under the bar are 지급, 사용, 결재 대기 and 소멸, a line is 지급, 월 지급, 이월, 직접 조정, 사용, 되돌림, 소멸 or 수당 정산, its columns 날짜, 내용, 일수, 사용 기한 and 설명, and the notes the plugin writes on a line (why the days came, went or were paid out) are rewritten the same way; the English is unchanged. A person's page reads their balances, requests and ledger down a wide column, with their terms and HR's forms beside it and the rarer ways folded into one pane. The filters of Announcements and People are the console's row of filters, the words first; an announcement opens in a column that starts where the panes do. A manager is shown by face and name on People; a request's time is written as every other day on the pages is; leave on the calendar is drawn as the console's calendar draws its bars; and a holiday stands out in a brighter red, its cell tinted and its date in red on the calendar, as is its date in every list of holidays and on the week's widget. The pages paint their figures, the bars, the dots, the calendar's chips and the cards' washes, with a palette of their own, clearer than the console's tones for words, which the words keep. Nothing the plugin does or stores changed.
