Breaking: built for console 0.4.6 or later. The pages Approvals and People and the widget Approvals are a manager's and HR's: the role `manager` follows who reports to whom in the console and is granted by nobody, so that someone who manages nobody no longer sees them. A decision reaches whoever it is for and the managers above them wherever they are, and the pages open a decision at its own address. Nobody is added to the board project any more, to request leave or to approve it: a request raises its decision there whether the requester may see the project or not, and they read that decision as the one who raised it. The queries `requests` and `request-view`, and the call `request-view`, answer a decision's address as `null` where the console does not show the caller the decision, so that a page offers no address that answers not found; the ids stay. The task to mend a line that a request found broken waits in the new collection `chores` until the daily job writes it, since a call for a person writes no task on the board. `setup` says that the workspace keeps UTC only where nobody set its zone. A day the calendar does not have is the console's to refuse. A link in an announcement to the web opens in a new tab, and one to the console goes there, by the console's whole address too.
