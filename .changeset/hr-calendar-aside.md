The calendar sets the month in the wide column with an aside beside it, under the month where the frame is narrow: the kinds of leave as the console's legend of a calendar, a dot of each kind's colour, with how a day is drawn under them, and the month's holidays. The month's name and the way to move it, back, this month and on, stand as the console's calendar has them, and today is marked as it marks it. Each person out on a day is the console's bar of a day in their kind's colour, their face at its start, dashed while the request waits; leave whose cancellation waits is still drawn, since it stands until it is decided.
