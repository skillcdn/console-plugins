The console's teams. The calendar's `team` is the people of a person's teams, or those who share their manager for someone in no team. An exception of the line may hold for teams, `"teams"` in `configure --rules` and a choice on the Setup page. An announcement may be for teams, `announcement --audience <team key>,...` and a choice in the editor, and only those teams and HR read it; `--audience everyone` makes it everyone's again. A team is named by its key, as `console teams` lists it, and a team the console removes stays named where it was, holding for nobody, until HR takes it off. The upgrade to `dataVersion` 2 gives what exists no team.
