Breaking: built for console 0.4.5 or later. The pages Approvals and People and the widget Approvals are a manager's and HR's: the role `manager` follows who reports to whom in the console and is granted by nobody, so that someone who manages nobody no longer sees them. A decision reaches whoever it is for and the managers above them wherever they are, so nobody is added to the board project to approve, and the pages open a decision at its own address. `setup` says that the workspace keeps UTC only where nobody set its zone. A day the calendar does not have is the console's to refuse. A link in an announcement to the web opens in a new tab, and one to the console goes there, by the console's whole address too.
