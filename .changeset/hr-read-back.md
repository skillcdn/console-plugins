The setting commands say what they saved. `setup` lists what the preset chose that organizations set differently, the year and its first period, the first year's monthly days, what is left at a period's end, how annual leave is requested and approved, the line and the time zone, for HR to compare with their rule before enrolling anyone; `policy`, `type` and `configure` read the policy, the kind and the line back in sentences, what a late request meets under each way of approving included; `enroll --leave on` says what it wrote, what is available, and where first-year monthly days came from. The skill `hr-administration` asks for the organization's rule first, sets it up in order and compares each answer with it: a rule of the whole organization is the policy's, and an adjustment is one person's.
