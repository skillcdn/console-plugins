A person's lines follow a policy that changed: `recount <login>` reverses the lines the policy wrote and no longer owes, or owes otherwise, with the expiries and payouts that closed them, each by a line of its own, and writes what the policy now owes. A line written with `adjust` is undone with `reverse <line> --note <why>`, and the ledger says which lines were reversed. Both are HR's, on a person's page too. Someone enrolled with first-year monthly days the organization does not give is set right with `policy <key> --monthly off`, then `recount <login>`, and `reverse` for each adjustment that offset those days.
