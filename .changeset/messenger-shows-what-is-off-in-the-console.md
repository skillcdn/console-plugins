My Slack notifications shows what is off in the console as off. A kind a member turned off in the console's own settings comes to Slack by no choice made in the messenger, and the page showed it as on all the same. It is dimmed now and not to be chosen there, with the choice made of it kept for when it is on in the console again, and the projects turned off in the console are named beside where the messages go. `notices` answers the same under `console` (the kinds, `projectsOff` and `ownAgents`, each the caller's own), and whether the caller is an agent account, to which nothing is sent; `notify` says of a kind turned on that it is off in the console. No capability is asked for it.
