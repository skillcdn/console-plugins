`status` says the next thing to do to set the messenger up, a step at a time: `next` is the first not done of a public address, the configuration token, the app, its installation, HR's role `hr` for the messenger (without which the shortcut works and no thread is begun), people with no email in the console (`noEmail` counts them) and a route, else `ready`; `text` says it in the caller's language, and the Connection page says it at its top. The README's "Enabling it" says beforehand what an installer has to decide or prepare: the app's names and icon, that installing the app is a person's step within fifteen minutes in the browser signed in to the console, how people are matched by their work email and what to do for those with none, which channels take a route, that the skill can be read before enabling, and that a secret is typed unseen at the terminal, never through an agent.
