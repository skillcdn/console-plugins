Built for console 0.4.3 or later. The answer to a request's or a cancellation's decision is read by the key the plugin gave the option, from the event itself, so that a label translated or trimmed cannot be misread; a decision raised before options carried keys is still read by its labels. The plugin hears when the console deactivates a person and turns their leave off from that day at once, with a task for HR to settle, and when a person who left comes back, with a task for HR to say whether their leave is on again; the daily job stays as the catch-up.
