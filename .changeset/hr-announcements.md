Announcements, for the first version of HR: what HR tells everyone, written with a toolbar (headings, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, highlight, lists, quotes, dividers, links) and with pictures, as a draft until it is published, by category, pinned for as long as HR says, on a page of their own, a widget in four sizes and the overview. An agent does all of it through the commands, as the person it works for may. The plugin now asks for the capability `files`, for the pictures. The configuration's `announcement` is gone: it is an announcement now.
