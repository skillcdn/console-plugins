A balance adds up when a person's past leave is entered after they were enrolled. An expiry or a payout written before the use counted for the days it said, though those days had already gone: the balance showed them as expired, the ledger listed them, and the year's export gave payroll more paid days than were closed. They now count for what they took, in all three, and the ledger leaves out one that took nothing. `carriedIn` includes what the period before left and still holds, the card on the leave page shows an adjustment, and what a person may take was right before and has not changed.
