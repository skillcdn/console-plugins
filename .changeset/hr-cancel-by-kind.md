Breaking: how approved leave is cancelled is each kind's to say, and the configuration's one rule goes, with `configure --cancellation`. A kind has a deadline of the notice's shape: none, some days before, a time the day before, or a time on the first day, in the workspace's zone. By it, the person cancels at once, at once up to some days and by a decision beyond them, by a decision, or not at all, HR alone; after it, through the first day, by a decision or by HR; once the first day has gone by, HR alone cancels, under every rule, since days were used. A decision goes to those the leave's first step asked, or to the person's manager where it was approved as written, and the person's own answer does not allow it. HR sets the rule on the Setup page, under Leave types, in a part of its own after the requests, or with `type <key> --cancel-notice --cancel --cancel-late`, which reads it back with the rest; a new kind, and each kind `setup` writes, is cancelled at once by the deadline its requests keep, or until 23:59 the day before where they keep none, so that no day begun is given back unseen, and by a decision after it. A request still waiting is cancelled at once, and HR cancels anything at once, as before.

The upgrade, to data version 3, writes on every kind what the configuration said, so that nothing changes by it but one thing. `free` is cancelled at once until 23:59 the day before and by HR from the first day, as it was; `hr` by HR alone; `approval` by a decision, except leave whose first day has gone by, which is now HR's to cancel rather than a manager's decision. Read each kind's rule on the Setup page after upgrading, and change what the organization does otherwise: "at once until 09:00 on the day, the manager after that, HR from the next day" is `--cancel-notice sameDay --cancel-notice-time 09:00 --cancel now --cancel-late decision`.

A request as the queries and the api answer it says what cancelling it would come to for its reader, `cancellation`: `now`, `decision` with `cancelDeciders`, whom the decision would ask, or `null` where they may not; `cancellable` stays. My leave says it before the cancellation is confirmed.
