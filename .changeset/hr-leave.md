The first version of HR, with leave, built for console 0.4.4 or later: requests from an agent or the page; lines of approvers as an organization draws them, step by step, with exceptions by kind, team, length and lateness, drawn from a person's own approvers, their team's, their manager in the directory, HR or people named; leave recorded by an approver for their people; per kind, the notice it asks, what becomes of a late request, approval as written and the part of a day it is taken in; balances from a policy (presets for Korea by hire date and by fiscal year, two US shapes, a flat number of days, or by hand) over a ledger of every change; holidays from built-in calendars for Korea and the US; a daily job that writes what is due and leaves HR tasks; what the console tells of people leaving or coming back; six pages and three widgets, and everything said in the language of whoever reads it. Attendance and the people's records come later in the same plugin.
