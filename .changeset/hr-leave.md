The first version of HR, with leave: requests from an agent or the page, approvals as decisions for the approver, balances from a policy (presets for Korea by hire date and by fiscal year, two US shapes, a flat number of days, or by hand) over a ledger of every change, holidays from built-in calendars for Korea and the US, visibility settings, a daily job that writes what is due and leaves HR tasks, six pages, two widgets and two skills. An administrator consents to `people:read`, `people:read:all`, `board:read`, `board:decisions`, `board:tasks`, `events:console` and `jobs`, binds a board project every approver is a member of, grants `hr` and `manager`, and HR runs `console plugin hr setup kr|us` before enrolling people.
