The first version of Messenger, built for console 0.4.6 or later: what the console's plugins have for people, told in Slack, and what people do there brought back. Whoever holds the role `messenger` connects the workspace's Slack from the Connection page, with the app configuration token and its refresh token, which an administrator pastes once: the plugin makes the app, the console seals its secrets, and someone who may install apps installs it from Slack's own page. People are matched to their accounts by email, within the hour and every day. For HR 0.2: a shortcut that opens the leave form in Slack, for the presser's own leave; each request threaded with the person and whoever decides, approved or rejected with a button that answers the console's decision for whoever pressed it, on their plate alone; approved leave told to the channels the Routes page names, in the person's name, and struck through when cancelled. The deployment needs a public https address Slack can reach, and outbound https to slack.com and hooks.slack.com; an administrator turns on `plugins:hr` and grants the messenger HR's role `hr` for the threads.
