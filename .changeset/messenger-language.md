The names the app shows in Slack, the leave shortcut's among them, are in the language of whoever connects it unless they choose another. 0.1.0 took the first of the console's languages, which is English on every console, so that a Korean workspace got "Request leave" unless 한국어 was chosen on the Connection page. A route reads in the language of whoever adds it unless they choose, and someone the console's pages have not seen yet reads the app's language, as do the problems the hourly job notes. An app made with 0.1.0 keeps its language: choose another on the Connection page and update the app.
