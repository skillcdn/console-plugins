The leave form in Slack follows the kind chosen: annual leave is chosen first where it is offered, as on HR's own form, and choosing another kind draws the form again for it, the days picked kept. A kind taken in whole days offers no part of a day, one taken in halves the two halves, one taken in quarters the halves and the four quarters; its reason is asked, optional or left out as HR says; and with HR 0.3.0 its rules are said under it in HR's own sentence. What the person has left stands at the top of the form, and someone HR keeps no leave for is told so in place of the form, since HR would refuse their request whatever the kind. A refusal is said beside the field it is about, by HR's code. The quarters of a day are named as HR names them: in Korean "오전 전반 반반차" to "오후 후반 반반차", in English "Early-morning quarter" to "Late-afternoon quarter", in the form and in the threads and the channels alike.
