The leave shortcut in Slack is named 휴가 in Korean and Time off in English, where it was 휴가 신청 and Request leave, and its form cancels leave as well as requesting it: above the fields it lists the person's leave from today on that HR says they may cancel themselves, the nearest five with a link to the rest on the console, each with a Cancel button. The button opens a second form over the first, which says what cancelling comes to by HR's rules (at once, or a decision and whose) and takes a reason; submitted, it cancels the leave for the person as HR's `cancel` does, says what became of it, and draws the list below again. The English name has no "leave" in it, since Slack's own `/leave` leaves the channel and Slack lists the two together. The cancellation of leave approved as it was written, which had no conversation, now opens one of the person and whoever decides it, so that the person reads what becomes of it there. The list and the rules need HR 0.3.0; with HR 0.2 the form requests leave as before. The app's manifest is written again within the hour of upgrading, with the new names; the shortcut keeps working meanwhile.
