The quarters of a day are named one way, on the pages, in decisions and in tasks: in Korean "오전 전반 반반차" to "오후 후반 반반차", beside "오전 반차", where the decisions said "반반차 오전 전반" and the pages "오전 전반"; in English "Early-morning quarter" to "Late-afternoon quarter", where "First quarter" read as a quarter of the morning.
